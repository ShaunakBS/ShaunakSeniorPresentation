## Cautions about the slide bullets
* The bullets are reasons the schools interest you. They do not claim you have joined any fund or workshop. Several of these (Kelley's Investment Banking Workshop, Miami's Cat 5 SMIF, Penn State's Nittany Lion Fund) are selective or competitive and open only after enrollment.
* If you are asked about the Investment Banking Workshop, you can say it is a selective program for admitted Kelley students.
